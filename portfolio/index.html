02Current work

Harmony AI

Brian is a founding engineer at Harmony AI and was one of its first hires. Harmony calls itself “the AI operating system for American manufacturing.” Its product is an AI layer on top of a plant’s existing machines, ERP and quality software, and paperwork. It connects them into one real-time system and automates scheduling, reporting and data entry, with no rip-and-replace.

Brian’s focus is production scheduling with machine learning and AI. Harmony sends forward-deployed engineers into plants, so much of the work happens on real factory floors.

Founded in 2025 and based in Chattanooga, Tennessee.

About this site

McKeown Industrial Works is Brian’s portfolio built as a small, explorable 3D factory. You steer an anonymous worker around a busy campus where five production lines feed a rocket assembly tower, and the stations around it hold the real portfolio:

  • Personnel Office: the résumé.
  • Manufacturing Control Room: Harmony AI, next to a wall of simulated factory telemetry.
  • Broadcast Booth: the Code Story interview.
  • Server Hall: GitHub projects.
  • R&D Annex: featured projects, each on its own test rig.
  • Recreation Yard: soccer and volleyball mini-games.

This page is the plain-HTML edition: the same facts with no 3D, and it works without JavaScript.

Enter the 3D factoryNeeds a browser with WebGL.

In-world fiction

McKeown Industrial Works is a fictional factory. The workers, the “CEO” mythology, production numbers, launch results and safety incidents are all jokes. The portfolio facts (résumé, Harmony AI, Code Story, GitHub projects, contact links) are real.
